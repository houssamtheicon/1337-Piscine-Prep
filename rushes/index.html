Rushes — Weekend Group Projects

Rushes are 48-hour collaborative projects done in pairs or small groups. They test teamwork, version control, and applying module concepts under time pressure.

Available Rushes

Tips for Rushes

Time Management

Divide the 48 hours wisely. Spend the first hour planning, split implementation into sessions, and reserve the final hours for testing and polishing.

Communication

Stay in constant contact with your partner. Use Discord, Slack, or sit together. Clearly communicate progress, blockers, and decisions.

Git Workflow

Commit early and often. Use meaningful commit messages. Pull before pushing and resolve conflicts immediately. Never force-push on shared branches.

Splitting Work

Divide the problem into independent modules. Work on separate files to avoid merge conflicts. Integrate and test each component together before submission.

Peer Evaluation Tips for Rushes

Rush evaluations are conducted in pairs and focus on collaboration as much as code. Here is what to expect:

  • Explain your approach: Walk your evaluator through your code structure and design decisions.
  • Show Git history: Your commit history demonstrates collaboration. Consistent, paired commits are a good sign.
  • Test edge cases: Be ready to run your project against unexpected inputs. Have test cases prepared.
  • Be honest about division of work: Acknowledge which parts each person handled. It shows teamwork awareness.
  • Ask for feedback: After presenting, ask your evaluator what they would improve. It shows growth mindset.
